- [x] **#11 — PSI "non-standard" epsilon-only-in-log variant — OVERSTATED, verified defensible (no formula change).** `stability.py:calculate_psi`/`calculate_csi_for_categorical`, `metrics.py:calculate_psi_by_period`. PSI = `Σ (a−e)·ln(a_safe/e_safe)` with epsilon flooring only the log term (difference keeps true proportions, no re-normalization). The audit's specific worry — *asymmetric weighting of appearing vs disappearing bins* — **does not hold**: PSI is invariant under swapping the two populations (`PSI(a,e)=Σ(a−e)ln(a/e)=PSI(e,a)`), and swapping turns every appearing bin into a disappearing one, so the two are weighted **identically** (numerically confirmed: appearing-mass-p == disappearing-mass-p, e.g. 0.6215==0.6215 at p=0.05). Fix (Option A, domain-expert-chosen): **keep the formula**, encode the verification — added `test_psi_symmetric_under_population_swap`, `test_psi_matches_textbook_within_epsilon`, `test_csi_symmetric_under_population_swap`, and a docstring note in all three functions. **Report-only** (PSI/CSI → CSV/JSON/HTML + INFO/WARNING logs + alert severities; `mr_pipeline` even downgrades errors to warnings; no optimizer/cutoff path consumes them).
